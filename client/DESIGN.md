## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines the drama, immersive scale, and editorial momentum of high-production media platforms for a luminous, sun-drenched travel booking marketplace. It shifts away from dark, heavy interfaces into an airy, Mediterranean-bright environment where clean architectural whites and warm slate surfaces let rich destination media shine.

The aesthetic is **Sunlit Modern Minimalist with Floating Glass Accents**. It relies on expansive `#FFFFFF` canvases, layered cool-tinted off-whites (`#F8F9FA`, `#F1F3F5`), crisp charcoal typography, and an electric, travel-inspired solar orange accent (`#FF5722` / `#F97316`). 

The brand evokes optimism, spontaneity, and elevated curation. Travel packages are framed as cinematic episodes—organized in horizontal shelf carousels, responsive media rails, and interactive billboard banners that feel light, tactile, and frictionless.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated for high sunlight legibility, clean visual segmentation, and punchy promotional energy:

- **Primary Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, clean white forms the dominant backdrop, creating an open atmosphere that lets trip imagery carry the emotional weight.
- **Surface Foundations (`#F8F9FA` & `#F1F3F5`)**: Subtle neutral tones used for shelf background bands, input wells, cards in resting state, and secondary containers.
- **Text & Contrast Tier (`#1E2022` & `#2D3134`)**: Deep charcoal slate establishes crisp typographic authority and effortless readability without the harshness of pure black. Supporting metadata uses `#4B5563` and `#9CA3AF`.
- **Primary Energy Accent (`#FF5722` / `#F97316`)**: Solar Orange serves as the system's interactive spark. It is applied to primary booking buttons, high-priority discovery tags, active carousel progress indicators, and verified badges.
- **Accent Glow Tint (`#FFF1EE`)**: A soft citrus wash applied to active tab pills, badge backgrounds, and selected card states.
- **Subtle Boundaries (`#E5E7EB`)**: Precise, low-contrast borders separate content zones without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography unites **Outfit** for cinematic headlines and promotional badges with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interface text, itineraries, price points, and booking inputs.

- **Headlines & Display (`Outfit`)**: Clean, circular geometric shapes deliver an editorial feel reminiscent of modern lifestyle publications. Tight negative letter spacing on larger sizes creates a crisp, composed appearance.
- **Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Humanist proportions, clear apertures, and distinct numeral forms ensure dense booking data (dates, durations, rates, seat availability) remains legible at all sizes.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`)**: All-caps tracking on small badges provides a neat streaming-catalog aesthetic for tags like "TOP RATED", "98% MATCH", or "NEW EXPERIENCE".

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances structural precision with fluid horizontal exploration:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with 56px (`3.5rem`) outer canvas margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Shelf rows contain 4 to 5 visible cards, deliberately breaking past the right boundary to encourage dragging and paging.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 32px margins and 16px gutters. Shelves surface 2.5 to 3 visible cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) margins and 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters. Shelf rails reveal 1.25 cards to signal horizontal scrolling.
- **Vertical Hierarchy**: Spacing between distinct category shelves is generous (`space-xl` / 40px) to prevent card fatigue and maintain a clean editorial cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Bright Tonal Stacking, Subtle Diffusion Shadows, and Translucent Glass Surfacing**:

1. **Canvas Surface (Level 0 - `#FFFFFF`)**: The foundational plain for page composition.
2. **Resting Cards & Rails (Level 1 - `#F8F9FA` / `#FFFFFF`)**: Cards use an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) coupled with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.02)`.
3. **Floating Controls & Search Docks (Level 2)**: Elevated search anchors and navigation bars use frosted glass filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), encased in a subtle outline (`1px solid rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.8)`) with shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.03)`.
4. **Active Card Expansion (Level 3)**: Media hover scales smoothly (`scale(1.04)`) with an orange-tinted depth glow: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.12), 0 0 20px -3px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.18)`.
5. **Modal Drawers & Overlays (Level 4)**: Detailed preview drawers and booking sheets sit over an airy backdrop: `background: rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.35)` with an `8px` Gaussian blur, accompanied by shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy employs a **Rounded (Level 2)** approach to convey modern polish and inviting tactile warmth:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Use `0.5rem` (`8px`) to maintain crisp architectural alignment across horizontal rails.
- **Search Docks, Expansion Panels, and Drawers**: Use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to produce soft container silhouettes.
- **Interactive Controls & Status Chips**: Use complete pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for search trigger buttons, filter vibe tags, match percentages, and CTA controls, differentiating them from media rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Instant Book" / "Explore Experience")**: Solid `#FF5722` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg` weight, pill shape. Hover shifts to `#E64A19` with a subtle orange glow shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost ("View Itinerary")**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E5E7EB` border and `#1E2022` text. Hover transitions to `#F8F9FA` with border color `#D1D5DB`.
- **Media Controls**: Circular 40px buttons with frosted glass backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), dark charcoal icons (`#1E2022`), and subtle lift on hover.

### Shelf Cards (Cinematic Marketplace Rails)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 for multi-day trips and scenic itineraries; 3:4 for city exploration and curated host spotlights.
- **Visual Structure**: Photography sits atop a crisp white lower information block. The lower zone houses the category, title in `#1E2022`, rating, price per guest, and duration tags.
- **Badges**: Anchored to the top-left of the image using a frosted white badge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) featuring `#FF5722` text.

### Search Dock
- Styled as a unified floating capsule divided into Destination, Dates, Experiences, and Guests segments.
- Segments are partitioned by subtle hairline dividers (`#E5E7EB`). 
- Active input regions highlight with a soft `#FFF1EE` wash and a `#FF5722` focus ring.

### Chips & Vibe Pills
- Resting: Background `#F1F3F5`, text `#4B5563`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- Active: Background `#FFF1EE`, text `#FF5722`, border `1px solid #FF5722`.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#1E2022`, rounded at `0.5rem`. Focus applies a `2px` ring in `#FF5722`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Unchecked states use `#E5E7EB` outlines; selected states fill with `#FF5722` featuring a clean white checkmark or radio dot.

### Quick Detail Tray (Media Drawer)
- An expandable full-width drawer that deploys inline beneath a shelf row when clicked.
- Features a split view: left side hosts an expansive 16:9 photography or video reel with travel itinerary highlights; right side provides an airy checkout card with pricing transparency, dates selector, and host credibility metrics.
